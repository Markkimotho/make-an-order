Features

Everything your team needs

A full-featured POS built for African SMEs — from cashier to pharmacist to stock clerk.

Point of Sale

Fast checkout with barcode scanning, fractional quantities, split payments, and real-time stock validation.

Shift Management

Open and close tills with opening floats, cash-in/out movements, and automatic Z-reports on shift close.

Pharmacy & Rx

Prescription capture, controlled-drug dispensing ledger, batch tracking and expiry date management.

Purchase Orders

Raise POs for suppliers, track delivery status, receive stock in partial batches and auto-update inventory.

Inventory Control

Full stock catalogue with SKUs, barcodes, reorder levels, low-stock alerts and movement history.

Loyalty & Credit

Staff-managed customer records with loyalty points, credit accounts and M-Pesa payment confirmation SMS.

Analytics & Reports

Sales summaries, cashier performance, top products, payment method breakdown and inventory valuation — with CSV export.

Role-Based Access

Six roles — Superuser, Admin, Cashier, Pharmacist, Stock Clerk and User — each seeing only what they need.

SMS & Notifications

Automated SMS receipts, low-stock alerts, prescription-ready notifications and OTP for manager overrides.

Payments

Accept every payment method

Getting started

Up and running in minutes

1

Register and log in. Your first login triggers the onboarding setup.

2

Choose your business type

Select pharmacy, supermarket, hardware, salon, restaurant or general retail. The system enables the right modules automatically.

3

A relevant inventory catalogue is seeded for your business type so you can start selling right away.
