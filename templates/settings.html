WhatsApp chatbot {% if t.wa_bot_enabled %}on{% else %}off{% endif %}

Customers message your own WhatsApp number, browse your stock, order and pay by M-Pesa without anyone at the counter. Orders land in Online orders; "talk to us" chats land in WhatsApp chats. You need a WhatsApp Business number on Meta's Cloud API.

How to connect your number (about 20 minutes, once)
  1. Go to developers.facebook.com/apps, create a Business app and add the WhatsApp product.
  2. Under WhatsApp > API Setup, add your business phone number and verify it by SMS. A number used in the WhatsApp Business app must be moved to the API, or connected with Meta's coexistence option.
  3. Copy the Phone number ID and WhatsApp Business account ID into the boxes below.
  4. In Meta Business Settings > System users, create a system user, give it the app and your WhatsApp account with full control, and generate a token with whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management. Paste it as the access token (it does not expire).
  5. In the app's Settings > Basic, copy the App secret and paste it below. Save.
  6. Under WhatsApp > Configuration, enter the webhook URL and verify token shown below, verify, and subscribe to messages.
  7. Press Check connection, switch the bot on, and send your number "Hi" from another phone.

On a shop server, the webhook needs the internet tunnel from the install guide (or a VPS), and M-Pesa must be set up above for the bot to take payments.
